Privacy

Pilot version. Last updated 12 November 2026.

ShrimpCount counts shrimp postlarvae from a photo of a tray. This page says what the app keeps, where, and how to have it removed. It is written in plain words on purpose.

Where your records are

On your phone, inside the app. If you sign in to a hatchery account, your records and the marked photos of your counts are also backed up to a private space that only the people in your hatchery's team can open. The person who runs ShrimpCount can see which hatcheries exist, how many people and counts each has, and the contact email on each account, for billing and support. We do not sell your data and we do not share it with advertisers.

When you count a photo

The photo is sent over a secure connection to our counting server, used for that one count, and not stored there. The marked photo you save stays on your phone, and in your hatchery's private backup if you have an account.

What we collect

  • If you make an account: your email address and the hatchery name you choose.
  • The counts, batch and order records and photos you save in the app.
  • Nothing else. There is no advertising, no tracking across other apps or websites, and no location collection.

The app asks for the camera only to take the photo of the tray.

The website and the app load fonts and icons from other companies' servers (Google Fonts, Fontshare and jsDelivr). Like any website that does this, those servers can see that your browser asked for them.

Deleting your data

Deleting a count in the app deletes it from your phone and, once the phone is online, from your hatchery's backup, including its photo. To have your whole account and everything backed up for it removed, email us from the address on the account and we will do it and reply to confirm.

Accuracy

The count is an estimate from a photograph. It can read low where larvae overlap in a packed tray, and it has been tested on only a few trays so far. Check the marks and compare with your own hand count before you rely on it for a sale.
